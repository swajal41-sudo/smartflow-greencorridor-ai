Scalability, Retrofit & Ethics Whitepaper

🏗️ 1. Scalability & City-Scale Deployment

SmartFlow I²TMS operates as a modular microservices architecture deployable on existing municipal ITMS hardware. Each intersection node computes Time-To-Intersection (TTI) and queue discharges at the local edge, enabling incremental scaling from a 4-junction pilot (Sitabuldi-AIIMS) to 200+ citywide junctions with sub-50ms coordination overhead.

💰 2. Cost & Retrofit Viability (Zero New Hardware)

100% software-driven deployment eliminates expensive physical transponder installations on civilian vehicles. The platform interfaces directly with Nagpur Safe City Project's 3,000+ CCTV cameras via RTSP streams, GPS tracking on 108 Emergency Ambulances, and existing SCATS/ITMS traffic controller relays via standardized REST/MQTT APIs.

🔒 3. Privacy & Ethics (DPDPA 2023 Compliance)

All citizen data adheres to strict zero-trust privacy principles: automatic license plate anonymization for non-violating vehicles, edge inference with zero permanent storage of raw CCTV footage, and aggregate density analytics for risk scoring. Fully aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023.

🇮🇳 4. Indian Mixed-Traffic Edge Cases

The system natively accounts for unregulated lane discipline, high proportions of two-wheelers (45%) and auto-rickshaws (25%), monsoon underpass waterlogging disruptions, VIP convoy protocols along Zero Mile, and religious/festival procession surges in dense old-city nodes like Itwari and Cotton Market.
