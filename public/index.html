SmartFlow Technical Architecture & Viability

🏗️ 1. Scalability & Nagpur Deployment

SmartFlow I²TMS operates as a modular microservices architecture deployable on existing municipal ITMS controllers. Each intersection computes Time-To-Intersection (TTI) and queue discharges locally, enabling smooth expansion from the 4-junction Sitabuldi-GMC corridor to 200+ citywide junctions.

💰 2. Cost & Retrofit Viability (Zero New Hardware)

100% software-driven deployment eliminates expensive physical transponders on civilian vehicles. The platform interfaces directly with GPS trackers on 108 Emergency Ambulances and existing SCATS/ITMS traffic controller relays via standardized REST/MQTT APIs.

🇮🇳 3. Indian Mixed-Traffic Physics

The simulation natively accounts for Indian Left-Hand Traffic (LHT), high proportions of two-wheelers and auto-rickshaws, civilian shoulder yielding, and multi-vehicle priority arbitration.
